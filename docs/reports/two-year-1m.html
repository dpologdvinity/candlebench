Each signal enters on the next bar's open with a stop at the pattern extreme padded by 0.100%, a target at 2.0R and a 20-bar holding cap, net of a quoted 1.85 bps per executed leg (every bar from observed quotes at its own time of day; priced per bar because the open runs several times midday). A bar touching both stop and target counts as a stop unless it opened past the target. Each pattern trade is compared, in gross R, with a random-entry control in the same session with the same direction and stop distance, entering one to five bars later. Intervals resample whole market dates; verdicts use Holm correction across all rows and 1 declared experiment(s), and a confirmed EDGE must also pass on held-out later dates.
